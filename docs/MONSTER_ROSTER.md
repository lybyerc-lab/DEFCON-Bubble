# Bubble Monster Roster

The approved visual source is [`concepts/bubble_monster_roster_v1.png`](concepts/bubble_monster_roster_v1.png). It defines one family: faceless enemies whose torsos, horns, joints, limbs, and feet are visibly made from soap bubbles rather than ordinary anatomy.

Left to right, the working roster is:

1. **Stiltwalker** - long-legged specialist; role not implemented.
2. **Brute** - ordinary pressure enemy; phone-accepted as BasicBubble in `[DB:ENCOUNTER:MIXED_THREE_WAVE]`.
3. **Runner** - urgent low-health enemy; phone-accepted as FastBubble at `2.25 m/s` with acid-lime presentation.
4. **Weaver** - support/summoner silhouette; role not implemented.
5. **Mutant** - asymmetrical specialist; role not implemented.
6. **Bubble King** - boss silhouette; role not implemented.
7. **Big Blub** - phone-accepted slow siege tank; five health, `0.55 m/s`, large target, and one castle IMPACT if it leaks.

Basic/Brute, Fast/Runner, and Big Blub were promoted with the phone-approved mixed three-wave encounter at `942083789e8d7dc4c61e9aad78757d95eb083451`.

Concept roles are not permission to invent mechanics early. Each new archetype still needs one bounded gameplay question, deterministic proof, and phone acceptance before its behavior becomes durable.
