# Game Director notes

## Purpose

This is the lightweight strategic layer between durable project law and the current task handoff. Read it in every fresh DEFCON BUBBLE chat after `AGENT_CONTEXT.md` and `docs/ACTIVE_HANDOFF.md`.

Keep this file short. It should preserve judgment, emphasis, risks, and creative/technical intent that would otherwise disappear when a chat is retired.

## Current notes

- The product target is unambiguous: DEFCON BUBBLE is a native mobile-first game. The phone is the battlefield and a real acceptance surface, not a later port target.
- Do not shrink the fantasy because the target is mobile. Keep the escalating beach-war spectacle and solve cost through staging, scalable presentation, controlled simulation, and measurement.
- Design from the thumb outward. Prefer direct, forgiving touch interaction that preserves battlefield visibility; do not default to virtual sticks or control clutter.
- Perceived scale matters more than brute-force object counts. Timing, layered action, silhouettes, destruction, sound, escalation, and smart staging should make the beach war feel enormous without treating raw simulation count as the goal.
- Desktop remains invaluable for development and automation, but do not let desktop convenience quietly dictate controls, readability, UI density, lifecycle behavior, or performance assumptions.
- Web preview is a review booth, not the finished building. Prefer exact-source GitHub-driven previews that are easy to open on a phone and easy to replace when hosting changes.
- Browser-on-phone evidence is excellent for rapid feel review; representative native-device evidence becomes required before player-facing behavior is considered platform-ready.
- Foundation, typed damage, the accepted soap-film POP, castle impact, First Defense, and the mixed three-wave encounter are now promoted on `main`.
- Treat accepted POP, castle consequence, and mixed-encounter behavior as reusable contracts, not standing invitations for more polish. Reopen them only for new player evidence, a measured defect, or production-scale requirements.
- The mixed encounter is phone-accepted. Fast Bubble reads as the urgent threat, the intermissions and final push are readable, one leak is recoverable, the second leak clearly loses, and RETRY returns a pristine encounter.
- The faceless bipedal bubble-creature family is accepted as the visual baseline. Silhouette, bubble-built limbs/horns, and gait carry identity without cartoon facial features.
- Basic/Brute, Fast/Runner, and Heavy/Big Blub are the first accepted roster interpretations. Big Blub is deliberately huge, five-hit, and slow. Do not collapse the approved seven-character lineup back into one generic bubble with stat changes.
- Environment presentation has its own reusable scene boundary. Grow the beach through composed environment pieces without letting scenery own enemy schedules, collision outcomes, castle health, or input.
- The next gameplay milestone is intentionally open. Do not silently choose the next monster, repair, upgrades, economy, another weapon, or broader wave framework. First define the next bounded player-facing question and phone acceptance gate.
- Protect the codebase from premature generalization. The accepted encounter proves authored escalation; it does not automatically justify a universal wave DSL, registry, navigation framework, or content grammar.
- Gameplay truth comes first; juice follows immediately after the truth works. Player feel remains a real gate.
- Prefer a proven, appropriately licensed asset when a commodity sound, image, font, or similar resource already meets the need. Reserve custom work for DEFCON BUBBLE's identity and project-specific problems; always record provenance and license.
- Meshy is available as a potential offline 3D asset forge. If used, DEFCON BUBBLE owns the approved exported assets; the shipped game must not depend on the Meshy service at runtime. API credentials stay out of Git, and important generated assets should retain provenance/generation notes where practical.
- Codex and AG can be used as specialist engineering/review partners, but repository law and Game Director acceptance remain authoritative.
- Keep the humble beach identity visible even as intensity grows. Serious systems should serve the absurd beach-defense fantasy, not sand off its personality.
- Do not invent device budgets or silently choose iOS versus Android launch priority before evidence and intent support locking them.
- New chats must verify live GitHub state before trusting metadata or stale prose. Contents, refs, commits, PRs, and CI are authoritative for implementation state.
- Continue the discipline: automation proves, the Game Director inspects evidence, the player judges feel, and promotion to `main` stays deliberate.

## Update discipline

Add or revise a note only when it would materially change how a future session should make a decision. When a note becomes a settled rule, move it into the appropriate decision/system document. When it stops affecting judgment, remove it.

Target size: roughly one screen to one page. This is a compass, not a diary.
